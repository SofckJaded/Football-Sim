- **Known Defects:** An independent audit (`docs/AUDIT_HANDOFF_2026-11-06.md`) reproduced 23 findings (F01–F23). **All 23 are implemented and reproduced-defect-free; the audit is closed.** Audit batches 1–21 cover: authoritative match state, keeper eligibility/parry/hold, shared pass planner, ball ownership + responder election + scoped reception assistance, persistent off-ball jobs, shot aiming/input single-source, the FIFA-style shooting model (power/height sweet spot, finesse, chip, trivela, strong/weak foot), execution-acknowledged metrics (contact-based pass/shot/completion counts, ownership-based possession, shared physical reach, input retry), live behaviour fields (shielding, per-role tendencies, team tempo/risk/freedom, tracked reaction delay, shot power), an enforced pre-match controller lock, Law 11 offside restart/contact semantics, authoritative boundary crossings with separate scorer/own-goal attribution, deterministic player contact geometry, one shared ball dimension for physics/render/contacts, time-based camera smoothing with bounded and diagnosed catch-up, fail-fast validation of the public match/pitch/attribute configuration, and a real recorded-command replay plus both-half/full-time completion and multi-seed tactical gates. The playtest passes then fixed five reported behaviours — AI carriers walking the ball out near a line, the first touch kicking the ball away, ball ping-pong (and camera shake) between two close players, a defence that only ever pressed with one player, and off-ball jobs assigned tens of metres from the player — and the follow-up tackle spam (8–9 alternating tackles between two close players, now 1–4 with the winner carrying the ball away). The latest follow-up fixed AI passing: a blocked pass line is now detected from the ball's flight and each opponent's run (the old static clearance skipped defenders standing on the ball) and abandoned, and pass power is anchored to a firm arrival pace instead of the slowest pass that merely reached — over 8 × 11v11 600 s, interceptions fell 26.8 % → 12.7 %, completion rose 73.2 % → 87.3 % and median pass power 65 % → 85 % with shots unchanged. **No reproduced P1 remains.** Phases 1–6 are closed. Residual limitations (goal-net approximation, subjective feel, no lock file, no physical-pad playtest, lower pass volume and shot counts than before the defensive/passing passes) are listed in `docs/KNOWN_ISSUES.md`; a green suite validates the simulated match and the presentation *timing*, not how it feels.
