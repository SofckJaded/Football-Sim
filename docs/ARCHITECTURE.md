# Architecture

1. **Strict Separation of Simulation and Presentation:** `FootballSim` targets `net8.0` with zero reference to `GodotSharp`. `MatchSimulationEngine` advances on a deterministic 60 Hz tick (`dt = 1/60s`).
2. **Coordinate System:** Meters and seconds. `X` = pitch length (`-52.5m` to `+52.5m`), `Y` = vertical height (`0m` ground), `Z` = pitch width (`-34.0m` to `+34.0m`).
3. **Unified Controller Contract:** `IFootballerController.Decide(in FootballerObservation, float dt)` emits a `FootballerCommand` struct representing movement, sprint, tackle, dive, and kick intentions. AI, Human, and Replay controllers share the exact same physical execution pipeline.
4. **Pre-Match Human Control Lock:** Configured via `PreMatchConfig.DesignatedHumanPlayerId`. The lock is a match invariant, not a check on the optional config: `MatchSimulationEngine.OverrideController` rejects unknown player ids and human controllers locked to a different player, and after tick 0 it rejects any human controller that would change which player the human drives (so a match created without a human can never gain one mid-match) as well as handing the locked player to a non-human controller. An accepted human controller becomes `LockedHumanController`, so live input, the responder election and spectator fast-forward always read the installed object. Red card, injury, or substitution transitions `HumanInputController` into spectator fast-forward mode.
5. **Game Shell:** `MainMenuNode` (`MainMenu.tscn`) owns Match setup (position + half length), Practice and Settings. Selections cross scenes through `GameSession.PendingMatch`; preferences persist via `GameSettings` to `user://settings.cfg` (resolution plus keyboard and gamepad bindings, schema-versioned). `HumanControlReader` is the single input read path for both the match and training scenes, backed by `ControlBindings` + `GamepadBindings`. Gamepad A activates the focused menu control (including OptionButton popups) and B goes back.
6. **Training:** `TrainingSession` (namespace `FootballSim.Training`) runs one `TrainingDrill` on the shared `BallPhysicsSolver`, `FootballerExecutionSystem` and `HumanInputController`, with optional AI participants supplied by the drill (`TrainingActorSpec`). A drill supplies spawn points, the attacking direction, goal / out-of-play predicates and optional per-tick rules; the session owns the respawn loop (goals settle the ball in the net, out-of-play resets). Practice and match share `BroadcastCamera` and the full `PitchVisualBuilder` map, but practice centres the trainee and has no HUD. `TrainingPasserController` drives the continuous Passing & Receiving rallies. `TrainingDrillRegistry` feeds the Practice menu, so new drills need no presentation changes. All of this lives in the Godot-free `FootballSim` assembly.
7. **Ball ownership and ball responder:** `BallContactEvent` classifies every play (`Kick` + `KickIntentType`, `Tackle`, `Collection`, `Save` + `IsParry`) and carries the planned receiver. `BallPossessionTracker` (`src/FootballSim/BallPossession.cs`) turns those events into `Loose` / `Controlled` / `InFlightPass` / `KeeperHeld` plus the controlling player, after the execution step and before the next tick's decisions. `TeamTacticalCoordinator.ObservePossession` confirms team possession every live tick from that model (control or a deliberate play; a deflection does not transfer it), and `BallResponderElection` elects one AI ball responder per team by time-to-reach with hysteresis. Controllers read all of this from `FootballerObservation`; the execution layer's reception assistance is scoped to the designated receiver of an in-flight pass.
8. **Held ball:** a goalkeeper clean catch sets `BallState.HeldByPlayerId` and `HeldReleaseDelaySeconds`. `BallPhysicsSolver` skips a held ball entirely, `FootballerExecutionSystem` attaches it to the holder's hands and refuses all contacts except the holder's own release kick, and `GoalkeeperController` runs the explicit `Positioning` / `Saving` / `HoldingBall` / `Distribution` / `Recovering` lifecycle. Restarts and training resets clear the hold; removing the holder releases the ball.
9. **Team jobs:** `TeamJobCoordinator` (`src/FootballSim/TeamJobs.cs`) assigns persistent off-ball jobs per team — in possession a near outlet, third-player option, far-side width, depth run, overlap, defensive cover and shape; out of possession a presser, cover defender, markers and a defensive line. Jobs are recomputed every six ticks, held for ~0.42 s, and interrupted immediately by a possession change; formation anchors stay the fallback. Each job exposes an anchor (and optional mark target) through `FootballerObservation`, and the AI scores the anchor plus a cover-shadow alternative on lane visibility, separation, travel, progress and offside eligibility.
10. **Input, aim and shooting:** `HumanControlReader` (Godot) is the only live-input path; it reads the saved keyboard/gamepad bindings, the first connected gamepad (`GamepadBindings.PrimaryDeviceId`), and — only when no gamepad is connected — an optional cursor aim point from `MouseAim` (camera ray intersected with the pitch plane). `HumanInputController` aims along the **movement direction**; the right stick is not read. Shots intersect that ray with the opponent goal line, clamped inside the posts; only zero directional input gets the assisted far corner. One kick button charges at a time. `ExecuteBallContact` resolves shots with a FIFA-style power curve (tap low, 80–90 % sweet spot, 100 % over the bar), optional finesse/chip/trivela modifiers, and a strong/weak-foot strike penalty based on the pre-contact body facing. The HUD help line and both scenes' aim arrows are generated from the same bindings and `LastResolvedAimDirectionXZ`.
11. **Execution-acknowledged metrics and reach:** `FootballerExecutionSystem.ContactAttempts` reports every requested kick/tackle with `Executed` and a refusal reason (`OutOfReach`, `BallTooHigh`, `FacingAway`, `InRecovery`, `LostArbitration`, `BallHeld`, `RestartTriggered`). `MatchStatisticsTracker` (`src/FootballSim/MatchStatistics.cs`) consumes only the authoritative `BallContactEvent`s: pass/shot attempts, pass completions (a team-mate controls the ball next, 6 s window) and the pass-in-flight lifecycle. Possession analytics read `BallPossessionTracker` (plus a separate `ControlledPossessionTicks`), not last touch. `BallContactRules` is the single physical reach/height/facing rule shared by execution and `HumanInputController`, and the engine feeds refusals back so a refused human press is retried for 0.22 s instead of being lost.
12. **Behaviour fields:** every advertised tuning field is read by the simulation. `BallContactRules.ShieldContestBonus`/`ShieldOpponentPenalty` (halved against tackles) and `ShieldSpeedFactor` make `FootballerCommand.ShieldBall` a real stance. `PlayerTendencies` (risk, direct passing, shooting appetite, dribble preference, positional discipline, pressing aggression) and `TeamTacticalProfile.Tempo01`/`RiskTolerance01`/`PositionalFreedom01` are applied as deltas around `PlayerTendencyBaselines` (neutral at default; role presets from `PlayerTendencyProfile.ForRole`). `PlayerAttributes.ReactionDelaySeconds` is a delayed but velocity-tracked ball perception (steady motion followed, changes noticed late; raw history without tracking made the AI chase a stale ball). `ShotPowerMax` scales shot speed, and kick accuracy uses the pre-contact body facing (`Align=…%` diagnostic).
13. **Rules and offside restarts:** `MatchRulesEngine` owns phase/period/clock and judges offside from the tick's `BallContactEvent`s. Restarts carry their Law 11 meaning in `RestartKind`: `ActiveRestart` is set when the restart is set up and `StampRestartOrigin` writes it onto the restart's **first contact** (the match engine calls it after execution, before `StepRules`), so the exemption cannot leak into open play. A player receiving directly from a throw-in, goal kick or corner is never offside (a kickoff, free kick or penalty snapshots normally); a keeper `Save` leaves an existing offside snapshot intact while a defender's deliberate kick/tackle/control ends it; and an offside offence restarts with an **indirect free kick** whose direct goal is a goal kick (or a corner for the taker's own goal) until another player touches the ball, whereas a foul's direct free kick can score straight from the kick.
14. **Boundary crossings and goal attribution:** `BallPhysicsSolver` records the first moment the **whole ball** crosses a touchline or goal line on its raw substep trajectory (`BallState.BoundaryCrossings`, planes offset by `BallPhysicsParameters.Radius`, cleared every `Step`), which is the only place the tick's intermediate geometry is visible. `MatchRulesEngine` classifies that first crossing — with `MatchRulesEngine`'s configured ball radius and whole-ball goal-mouth test — and falls back to the endpoint only for a ball moved without physics, so a dipping shot is judged at the bar, a corner exit keeps its event order (touchline first → throw-in) and a post rebound is nothing. The restart spot is the crossing point. Goals carry `ScorerPlayerId` / `OwnGoalPlayerId` / `IsOwnGoal` on the `MatchRuleEvent`, computed from the last touch plus the tracked shot (a shot survives a `Save`), while `PlayerId` remains the physical last toucher.
15. **Presentation timing, shared dimensions and validation:** `BallGeometry` (`src/FootballSim/BallPhysics.cs`) is the one source of the ball's size — `BallPhysicsParameters.Radius` defaults to it and both scenes draw the sphere and its shadow from `engine.BallRadius` / `session.BallRadius` through `SphereRadius`, `Diameter`, `ShadowRadius` and `RestingCentreHeight`, so apparent contact matches physical contact. `FrameTiming.cs` owns the presentation clock: `FixedStepClock` turns rendered frame deltas into at most `MaxStepsPerFrame` fixed steps, truncates frames longer than `MaxFrameDeltaSeconds` and reports `CappedFrames`/`DroppedSeconds` (the HUD shows a capped catch-up); `CameraSmoothing` replaces per-frame lerps with a half-life in seconds (`MatchHalfLifeSeconds` is derived from the legacy 0.14 @ 60 Hz weight, so 60 FPS feel is unchanged at any frame rate). `SimulationConfigValidation` (`src/FootballSim/SimulationConfigValidation.cs`) validates the public match config, pitch geometry, ball parameters, player attributes, tendencies and team profiles before construction, and `MatchPositions.ResolveInspectedPlayerId` resolves the HUD inspector id against the real squad instead of indexing by id. `MatchClientNode` / `FreeTrainingNode` also clamp inspector-supplied zoom, so scene settings cannot create an invalid simulation.
16. **Reproducible coverage:** `ReplayAndCoverageTests` records every decided command from a live AI match and replays it into two interleaved fresh engines, asserting every tick's ball, player, score, phase and clock (a real replay, unlike the same-seed determinism gate), completes a short match through `HalfTime` to terminal `FullTime`, and gates the tactical profiles across four complete seed-set matches with execution-aware kick counts. The headless CLI (`src/FootballSim.Headless`) exits non-zero when a match does not reach full time or a tactical gate fails, so console output cannot claim a completion the engine never reached.
17. **Ball control, carries and the defensive press (playtest pass):** `FootballerExecutionSystem.ExecuteBallContact` Case 3 owns the ball at a player's feet. A first touch retains only `(1 − control quality) × 30 %` of the incoming pace and may nudge the ball ≤0.15 m onto the foot; a dribble keeps it with a spring capped at player speed + 2.6 m/s and a ≤0.10 m correction; a won tackle keeps 55 % of the tackler's run. A `Collection` sets a 0.12 s exclusive control claim that only a deliberate kick or a tackle bypasses, and team-mates do not plain-touch a ball a team-mate is still on, so two players cannot flick the ball between their feet. `TacticalUtilityAiController.ScoreCarryDirection` scores carries on open space **and** pitch-boundary safety, rejects directions that would take the player or the ball out, and clamps the carry target 1 m inside the lines. On defence the elected responder pursues the carrier's future position and arrives goal-side; one central defender (CB/DM) takes a covering position once the carrier is inside the last 20 m. `TeamJobCoordinator.MaxAssignmentTravel` (18–26 m for contextual jobs) stops contextual jobs pulling players out of formation, and `BroadcastCamera` derives its look target from the smoothed camera position (never the raw frame focus), so a contested ball cannot shake the view. A tackle winner gets a 0.4 s shield (cleared by their own deliberate kick), recovers in 0.08 s at 55 % speed (the touch lock matches); the dispossessed player is stunned 0.55 s, and `TacticalUtilityAiController` has a 0.55 s tackle cooldown. Without that restraint two close AI players traded the ball with 8–9 alternating tackles in 7 s.
