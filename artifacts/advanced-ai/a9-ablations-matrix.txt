Simulation version : ai52-rs1
Ablation model     : an ablation is the off build of one mechanism at the same seed, the same
                     squads and the same ends: both builds run the same engine, profiles, rules
                     and cadence, and the only difference between them is one AiRuntimeConfig
                     switch. Whatever differs between the two matches is therefore what that
                     mechanism is worth, not a retuning of it. The reference build is
                     new AiRuntimeConfig() (every switch on).
Divergence         : a sampled tick diverges when the two builds' ball positions differ by more
                     than 0.05 m or the score differs; the first divergence
                     tick is the first such sampled tick, and mean/max ball separation is over the
                     common tick range of the two matches.
Classification     : mechanical, from the measured numbers: inert when the mechanism's reference
                     counter is zero or no paired seed diverged at all; overwhelming when on at
                     least seeds-1 paired seeds |possession delta| >= 10.0 pp OR
                     |goals delta| >= 2 OR |ticks delta| >= 15 % of the
                     reference ticks, AND the mean ball separation is >= 3.0 m;
                     otherwise measured. The CLASS column is that rule's output, not an opinion.
