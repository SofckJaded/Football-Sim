=== A9.1 style-matrix evidence: six presets vs the neutral benchmark, 8 seeds x 360 s, home and away ===
Simulation version : ai52-rs1
Style matrix       : every preset plays the neutral benchmark profile (new TeamTacticalProfile(),
                     "Balanced") once at home and once away on the same seed, so the venue and
                     end effect cancel in the style's mean. The six presets are preference bundles
                     on one decision pipeline: the same squads, rules and AI cadence, with no
                     candidate, sight, reach or contact differing between them.
Presets            : Possession = Possession / Positional Play | Direct = Direct | Counter = Direct Counterattack
                     Press = High-Press Gegenpressing | LowBlock = Low Block | ManOr = Man-Oriented Press
Metrics            : possession share = the style's possession ticks / (home + away possession
                     ticks); passes/shots/goals = the style's own executed outcomes; width = the
                     possession-scoped span; territory = mean ball x in the style's attacking
                     direction while in possession (a ball-progress measure - printed, not gated);
                     markers = style players whose live job is TacticalJob.Marker per 30-tick
                     sample, body distance = marker-to-marked-man XZ distance including the
                     approach lag (printed, not gated: it is not the assignment-time standoff).
Line height        : mean of DefensiveLineBand.AnchorMeanProjX over the 30-tick samples in
                     which the style team is defending and the band is valid, in metres in the
                     style's own attacking direction (positive = higher up the pitch). The band's
                     placed depth ProjX is reported beside it but not gated: caps and local
                     exceptions are situational, the anchor mean is the profile's own line.
Verdict            : the predefined claim block at the end, paired by seed. Two kinds of claim,
                     because they are two kinds of statement: a SHAPE claim (width, line height,
                     marker count) is a property of the assignment the presets' numbers state, so
                     it must hold on all but at most one seed; a CONSEQUENCE claim (possession
                     share) is a stochastic match outcome, so it needs a paired-seed majority and
                     a mean threshold. Pass volume, completion, territory and marker body
                     distance are reported, never gated - the three-seed diagnostic and the
                     recorded eight-seed run both showed they are not monotone in style (a direct
                     side's longer balls still count as passes, and a turnover raises the count).
